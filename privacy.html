Goalkeeper Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Goalkeeper ("Goalkeeper", "we", "our" or "us") handles information when you use the Goalkeeper web application and its related community features.

1. Information we may process

  • Telegram information: When you open Goalkeeper through Telegram, the app may receive Telegram Mini App session data and, where provided and verified by our backend, basic Telegram account identifiers and profile information.
  • Wallet information: If you choose to connect a TON wallet, the app may process the public wallet address and connection status. Goalkeeper does not need your wallet seed phrase or private key.
  • Activity information: Mission status, testnet points/XP, streaks, referrals, event participation and eligibility records may be processed to operate the app and keep activity consistent.
  • Device and technical information: Your browser may store app preferences and test/fallback state in local storage. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process standard technical logs such as request time, IP address and error information.
  • Messages you send: If you contact support, we process the information you include in your message.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide and maintain Goalkeeper, verify Telegram Mini App sessions, connect a wallet when you request it, record missions and community activity, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, respond to support requests and protect the service.

3. Wallets, blockchain and testnet activity

Wallet connection is optional. A public wallet address may be visible to blockchain services when you use wallet-related features. Blockchain records can be public and may not be editable or removable by us. Goalkeeper's current project documentation describes its wallet experience as TON testnet-oriented and says it does not request payments. Points are testnet activity points and have no cash value. NFT minting and token payments are not currently enabled.

4. Pi Network features

Pi Network integration is being evaluated and developed separately. Pi login, Pi payments and Pi account data are not represented as active features by this policy unless and until they are implemented and clearly made available in the app. If Pi features are launched, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data flows before those features are used.

5. Service providers and sharing

Goalkeeper relies on third-party platforms to operate, which may include Telegram, TON wallet/blockchain infrastructure, GitHub Pages or other website hosting, and the Goalkeeper backend hosting provider. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or to investigate abuse or security incidents.

6. Storage and retention

Some preferences and test/fallback information may remain in your browser's local storage until you clear the app's site data. Server-side activity records may be retained for as long as needed to operate the service, maintain security and resolve disputes, or as required by law. Retention periods may vary by data type and provider.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to a developing web application. No online service or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Never share your wallet recovery phrase, private key, passwords or one-time authentication codes with anyone claiming to represent Goalkeeper.

8. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to connect a wallet and can stop using the app. You can clear locally stored app data through your browser settings. To ask a question or request access, correction or deletion of information that we control, contact us using the email below. We may need to verify your request and may retain limited records where required for security or legal reasons.

9. Children's privacy

Goalkeeper is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Goalkeeper's features or legal requirements change. The revised date at the top of this page indicates when it was last updated. Continued use after an update means the updated policy is available for review; it does not remove any rights you have under applicable law.
